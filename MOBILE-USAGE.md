# Mobile Usage Guide - Real Estate Champ

## 📱 Easy Property Management on Your Phone

Real Estate Champ is designed to be **mobile-first**, allowing realtors to quickly add property photos and information directly from their phones while on-site.

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

### 1. Install as Mobile App (PWA)

**On iPhone/iPad:**
1. Open Safari and go to your Real Estate Champ URL
2. Tap the Share button (📤)
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add" - the app icon will appear on your home screen

**On Android:**
1. Open Chrome and go to your Real Estate Champ URL
2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
4. Tap "Install"

### 2. Sign In
- Open the app from your home screen
- Sign in with your realtor account
- You're ready to start adding properties!

### 3. Add Your First Property
1. Tap the **"+ Add Property"** button
2. Take photos with your camera or select from gallery
3. Fill in basic details (title, address, price, etc.)
4. Tap **"Create Property"**
5. Your property is saved instantly!

## 📸 Taking Great Property Photos

### Mobile Camera Tips:
- **Good lighting**: Take photos during daytime or with lights on
- **Wide shots**: Show the full room, not just details
- **Multiple angles**: Capture each room from 2-3 different angles
- **Clean spaces**: Remove clutter before photographing
- **Highlight features**: Focus on unique selling points

### Photo Workflow:
1. Visit the property
2. Open Real Estate Champ app
3. Tap "+ Add Property"
4. Tap "Take or Upload Photos"
5. Grant camera permission (one-time)
6. Take photos room by room
7. Review and delete any bad shots
8. Add property details
9. Submit!

## ✨ Generate Content with AI

Once you've added photos and basic info:

1. Open your property

3. Wait 10-30 seconds
4. AI creates:
   - Facebook post

   - Blog article

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working?
1. Go to phone Settings
2. Find Real Estate Champ app
3. Enable Camera permission
4. Reload the app

### Upload Failing?
1. Check internet connection
2. Photos may be too large (max 10MB each)
3. Try uploading one at a time
4. Contact support if issues persist

### App Not Updating?
1. Pull down to refresh
2. Close and reopen app
3. Clear browser cache
4. Re-add to home screen
